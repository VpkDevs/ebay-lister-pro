ListAI Pro removes the desk work from reselling. A seller photographs an item; the app's vision AI identifies it, extracts specifications and defects, writes a complete listing (title, description, item specifics), and suggests a price grounded in live sold-listing comps — showing the evidence behind every number. The seller reviews the draft and publishes to eBay in one click, with cross-posting to Shopify, WooCommerce and Etsy and export files for Mercari and Poshmark.
Plans are metered by completed generations: Pro ($19/month, 300 listings) and Power Seller ($49/month, 1,000 listings, team seats). A free tier gives 10 listings a month when the seller supplies their own free Google Gemini API key, which keeps the free tier free to operate. ListAI Pro is built and run by solo founder Vincent Kinney in San Antonio, Texas, and was audited end-to-end before launch — its first launch date was blocked by its own audit until real billing-security holes it found were fixed.
