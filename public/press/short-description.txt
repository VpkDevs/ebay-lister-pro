ListAI Pro is a subscription web app for eBay resellers. Upload a product photo and it identifies the item, writes the title, description and item specifics, suggests a price from live sold comps, and publishes to eBay — with cross-posting to Shopify, WooCommerce and Etsy. Pro is $19/month for 300 listings; a free tier offers 10 listings a month on your own Gemini key.
