Frequently Asked Questions

ListAI Pro — the top 20, answered plainly

1. What counts as one listing?

One completed AI generation. Edits are free, and a generation that fails is never counted. Your counter only moves when you actually receive a listing.

2. How does the free tier work?

Free accounts get 10 generations a month, and they run on your own Google Gemini API key (free from Google AI Studio). Save your key in Settings → the app shows it masked and never displays it in full. Without a saved key, free accounts can't generate — that's what keeps the free tier free to operate.

3. How do I get a Gemini API key?

Go to Google AI Studio (aistudio.google.com), sign in, and choose 'Get API key'. It's free. Paste it into ListAI Pro under Settings. The in-app guide walks you through it.

4. Where does the suggested price come from?

Live sold-listing comparables for the item the AI identified. The price always arrives with the comps behind it — open the panel and check the evidence before you trust the number.

5. Which marketplaces can I publish to?

eBay directly. Shopify, WooCommerce, and Etsy are built-in cross-posts. Mercari and Poshmark don't offer real posting APIs, so ListAI generates formatted export files for them instead of pretending to post.

6. Is my eBay account safe?

We connect through eBay's official OAuth — never your password — and nothing publishes without you approving the draft. There is no auto-publish mode.

7. What's the refund policy?

Subscription fees are non-refundable except where the law requires it. Duplicate or erroneous charges are always refunded in full. If the service is materially broken in your first 7 days and we can't fix it, contact support for a refund of that first payment. Full text: Terms of Service, section 4.

8. How do I cancel?

Dashboard → Billing → Cancel, or through the Stripe billing portal linked there. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the paid period; you keep access until then. No email-to-support required.

9. Do unused listings roll over?

Plan allotments are per calendar month and reset monthly. (If rollover matters to you, tell support — it's the most-requested policy question and it's under active consideration.)

10. What are team seats?

Power Seller includes team seats so up to 3 people can work in one workspace. Invite from the Team section; members you invite get their own sign-ins.

11. I'm on a paid plan and hit my cap. Now what?

You can plug in your own Gemini key (Settings) and keep generating past the cap — generations on your key aren't counted against your plan. Or upgrade; Pro is 300/month, Power Seller 1,000.

12. What happens to my photos?

They're processed by Google Gemini to build your listing and, when you publish to eBay, temporarily hosted (tmpfiles.org / file.io) so eBay can fetch them. The Privacy Policy names every processor — no vague 'service providers' language.

13. Do you train AI on my listings?

No. Your content is processed to serve you and is not used to train models by us.

14. The AI got my item wrong. What now?

Regenerate with a clearer photo of the label, tag, or serial plate — identification drives everything downstream, including price. And edit the draft freely; it's yours.

15. A generation failed. Was I charged a listing?

No. Only completed generations count. If failures repeat, check the error code shown — a rejected API key (yours or expired) is the common cause, and the message says so plainly.

16. How does the Chrome extension work?

It scrapes the product page you're viewing and saves it as a draft in your ListAI dashboard. It needs your server's operator API key: extension Settings → paste the API_KEY from your server .env. The extension ships with no built-in key.

17. Can I import my existing inventory?

Yes — CSV import and batch generation are built in. See the dashboard's Import section for the column format.

18. How do I delete my account and data?

Dashboard → delete account, or call DELETE /api/user/account. It removes your profile, subscription and usage records, saved API key, and team data.

19. Who runs ListAI Pro?

One person: Vincent Kinney, a solo founder and practicing reseller in San Antonio, Texas. Support reaches him personally.

20. How do I reach support?

Through the support contact published in the dashboard. Response target: within 2 business days, usually much faster — and the person answering wrote the code.
